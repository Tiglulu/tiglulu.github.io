What Otta is

Otta is a workout tracker. You log workouts, sets, weights, reps, machines and their settings, routines and training plans, and Otta shows them back to you as history, records and charts. Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account and no server holding your training data. How the app handles data is in the privacy policy, at the address shown in Settings, About.

Health and safety

Otta is not medical advice and not a medical device. It makes no recommendations: it does not choose your weights, sets or exercises, and it does not change them for you.

Numbers Otta calculates, such as volume or an estimated one-rep max, are arithmetic on what you entered. They are estimates, not targets, and not a judgement of what is safe for you to lift.

Strength training carries a risk of injury. Use proper technique, warm up, use a spotter or safety equipment where it is wise, and stop if something hurts. Talk to a doctor before starting or changing an exercise programme, especially if you are pregnant, injured or have a health condition. Train within your own limits; you are responsible for what you lift.
