Otta privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27

Otta is a workout tracker for Android, published by Tiglulu, the developer name of Tiago Afonso, an individual developer in Portugal, who is responsible for the data described here. This page says what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and what you can do about it. Questions: tigs.info.contact@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your workouts, machines, routines and settings stay on your phone. We run no server, no account system and no analytics, and we cannot see your training data.
  • The only thing Otta sends over the internet by itself is purchase information, to handle Otta Pro. That goes to RevenueCat and Google Play.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

What Otta stores on your phone

Everything you enter: workouts, sets, weights and reps, machines and their settings (seat height, cable notch and so on), routines, training plans, notes, and app settings. It is kept in a database inside the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Otta does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account, and does not use your camera, microphone or files.

Notes and anything else you type can include details about your health, such as an injury. Otta never asks for health information and does not need it. Like the rest of your data, it stays in the app on your phone unless you export it, share it, or Android backs it up.

What leaves your phone

Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play)

Otta has a free tier and an optional paid tier, Otta Pro. To sell and check it, Otta uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, together with Google Play Billing.

  • When the app starts, and when you open the paywall, buy or restore a purchase, Otta contacts RevenueCat.
  • RevenueCat identifies you by a random anonymous ID created on your device. Otta does not give it your name, email or any account.
  • RevenueCat receives your purchase information from Google Play (which product, whether it is active, when it renews or expires) and, as with any internet request, your IP address and basic technical details such as app version, device type and language. This exists only to unlock Pro and to let you restore it after a reinstall.
  • RevenueCat acts for us as a service provider. See its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy for how it handles data, including where it is processed.
  • Google Play handles the payment itself under Google's own privacy policy. Otta never sees your card or payment details.

Otta never sends your workout data to RevenueCat or Google Play.

Data outside the EU

RevenueCat is based in the United States, so the purchase information above may be processed there, outside the European Economic Area. That transfer is covered by the safeguards in RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum. To ask about these safeguards, write to tigs.info.contact@gmail.com.

Android backup

Otta allows Android's automatic backup. If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy Otta's data, including your workout database, to your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is done by Android, not by Otta, and is covered by Google's privacy policy. You control it in your phone's Settings under Google, Backup. Turn it off there to stop it, and delete the backups from your Google account.

Exports and sharing

Otta can export a routine or a full backup file. A file is created only when you ask, and it goes wherever you choose to send it through Android's share sheet. Otta does not send it anywhere itself; once you share it, the app you share it with handles it. Importing a file reads it on your phone only.

Feedback emails

Settings has Send feedback. It opens your own email app with a message to tigs.info.contact@gmail.com, filled in with the Otta version, whether you have Pro, your phone's make and model, its Android version and the app's language. You can read and change all of it, and nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. If you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use them only to answer you and fix the app, and delete the email when it is no longer needed for that.

Notifications

During a workout Otta shows an ongoing notification with the workout name, set count and volume. When a rest ends while Otta is in the background, a second notification, "Rest over", shows the workout name and buzzes. Both are made on your phone and visible on the lock screen, so anyone who can see your lock screen can see them. You can turn either off in Android's notification settings for Otta.

Permissions

What each permission Otta asks Android for is used for:

  • Internet: only to reach RevenueCat and Google Play for purchases, as above.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Otta Pro.
  • Network state: Google Play's billing library checks whether you are online before it talks to Google Play.
  • Notifications: the workout and "Rest over" notifications above.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to buzz you at the end of a rest when your phone is locked, where the app's own timer is paused. The alarm is set on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. If you do not allow it, the rest simply ends without a buzz.
  • Vibration: short buzzes when you log a set, when a rest ends and when you set a record.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

The purchase check is needed to deliver and restore Pro, which is performance of the contract you enter when you buy it. If you email us, we use your address and message to answer you, which is our legitimate interest in supporting the app you use. Your training data stays on your device and is not processed by us.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, uninstall Otta or clear its data.
  • At RevenueCat: the purchase record tied to your anonymous ID is kept under RevenueCat's own retention, and may be needed for tax and accounting. To ask for it to be deleted, write to tigs.info.contact@gmail.com.
  • In an Android backup: until you delete the backup from your Google account.
  • A feedback email you send: only as long as needed to answer it and fix what it reports.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete it all: uninstall Otta, or use Android Settings, Apps, Otta, Storage, Clear data. This removes the database and settings from your phone.
  • Export it: Settings in Otta has an export that gives you a file of your data.
  • Purchase data held by RevenueCat: write to tigs.info.contact@gmail.com to ask for access, correction or deletion. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies you also have the rights to restrict and port your data, to object, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because there is no account and the anonymous ID lives only on your phone, we may need your Google Play order number to find your purchase record.
  • Cancel a subscription: in Google Play, under Subscriptions.

Children

Otta is not aimed at children under 13 (or the age set by your country) and does not knowingly collect their data. It collects almost nothing from anyone.
